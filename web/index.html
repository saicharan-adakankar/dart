About DART

DART (Download And Retrieve Tool) saves media from supported links and direct file URLs. Select a source and download type, paste a link, then choose from the available quality and format options.

Video and audio

Download the best available video quality or choose a lower resolution. Keep the source format, convert to MP4 or WebM, or save source streams in MKV without re-encoding. Extract audio as MP3, M4A, or WAV when audio is available.

Photos and profile pictures

Save the source image unchanged with Original, or convert PNG, JPEG, and WebP inputs to those formats. Conversions use the first frame. Profile-picture resolution depends on what the platform exposes; Instagram lookup can fail or return only a thumbnail, for public and private accounts.

Source quality and availability

DART selects from the sources it can retrieve. Conversion cannot restore missing detail. Platform selection does not guarantee support for every link or download type. Login-only media, DRM-protected content, and photo galleries are not supported.

Files and storage

Direct files retain their existing format. DART sets no file-size cap; available disk space still limits downloads. Completed files are kept for up to one hour. New link discards the previous job and its file, and active downloads can be cancelled.
