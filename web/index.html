A little more about DART +

Video: public videos through yt-dlp. Available resolutions come from the source; the best video and audio are merged without re-encoding.

Photo: direct images or the main image exposed by a public page. This may be a compressed preview; galleries aren’t supported yet.

Profile picture: direct avatar links or pages exposing explicit public avatar metadata. Some social profiles do not provide this without login.

File: direct HTTP/HTTPS file links. Files stay on this server for up to one hour, then are deleted. Restarting clears them too.
