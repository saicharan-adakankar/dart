A little more about DART +

Keep it original. Choose Original to keep source streams or image bytes. MP4/WebM and audio conversions re-encode; MKV changes the container without re-encoding.

Your format, your call. PNG, JPEG and WebP conversion supports PNG/JPEG/WebP inputs and uses the first frame. Other image types can be saved as Original. General files keep their existing type.

Profiles and photos. A public page may expose only a compressed preview. Private accounts can work when their avatar is publicly visible. Instagram lookup uses account-matched page data and direct account-info requests. HD access may require an optional local Instagram session; blocked requests remain unavailable. Galleries aren’t supported yet.

A short stay. Completed files stay on this server for up to one hour. New link discards the previous file. Available disk space is the practical download limit.
